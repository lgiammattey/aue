Leia primeiro, sempre: [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md), [`docs/jogo/VISAO.md`](../../docs/jogo/VISAO.md) e a skill [`google-search-console`](../../.agents/skills/google-search-console/SKILL.md).
